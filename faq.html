Here are the questions people ask before they download Local Meeting Manager.

If something you need is not answered below, you can reach the maker directly by email and ask.

Does a bot join my meeting?
No. Local Meeting Manager records your Mac's own microphone and system audio directly, so no third-party attendee ever appears in the call. The other participants see the same meeting they always would.
Where is my meeting data stored?
On your Mac. Recordings sit in a local audio folder, transcripts and notes live in a local SQLite database, and any API keys are kept in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on a cloud AI feature.
Can I use Local Meeting Manager fully offline?
Yes, if you use the local model. Recording and on-device transcription never need the internet, and the local AI runs over localhost, so summaries and chat work with no connection at all.
What does it cost?
The app is a one-time $9.99 purchase, and you can record your first ten meetings free before you buy. The AI is separate and yours to choose: a free local model costs nothing to run, while your own Claude API key runs about five to eight cents for a 30-minute meeting.
Do I need an account to use it?
No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no Local Meeting Manager server, so you download the app and start recording.
Which meeting apps does it work with?
It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, and others. Detection covers both native apps and browser tabs, and you can always start an ad-hoc recording when a call is not on your calendar.
Why does macOS warn me on first launch?
The app is not yet notarized with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS shows a Gatekeeper warning the first time. Right-click the app and choose Open once, confirm, and you will not see the warning again.
Do I have to send my meetings to the cloud?
No. You can run a local model so that no meeting content ever leaves your Mac. A cloud summary happens only if you add your own Claude API key and choose the cloud option, and even then only the relevant transcript slice and your prompt are sent.
Is there a Windows or iOS version?
No. Local Meeting Manager is a native macOS app, and there is no iOS, Windows, web, or Linux version today.
Does it work with Outlook?
Yes. Use Apple Calendar as the source and your Outlook for Mac events appear through macOS, alongside iCloud and other calendars.
How accurate is speaker identification?
It improves as it learns. The first time a new person speaks the app may guess, but once you rename them their voice is remembered, so the next meeting is labeled correctly on its own.
How is it different from cloud notetakers like Otter, Fireflies, or Granola?
Those tools are cloud services that typically send a bot into your call, store your audio and transcripts on their servers, and bill a subscription. Local Meeting Manager captures the call from your own Mac, keeps the audio and transcripts on your device, needs no account, and is a one-time purchase.
