How it works

Local Meeting Manager takes your call from recording to summary in five steps on your Mac.

Most meetings finish this pipeline within two to five minutes of when you stop recording.

1

It captures both sides of the call.

The app records your microphone and your system audio locally and mixes them to a file on disk. It can start automatically when it detects a call window, from a calendar event, or as an ad-hoc recording.

2

It transcribes the recording on your Mac.

WhisperKit converts the audio to text on-device, so nothing is uploaded to be transcribed. The first meeting downloads the model once, and later meetings start instantly.

3

It works out who said what.

The app clusters voices and maps each one to a real name using voice fingerprints, calendar attendees, and spoken-name cues, and it learns each voice so the next meeting is right automatically.

4

It writes the summary and the follow-ups.

It produces a structured summary, extracts action items with assignees and due dates, drafts a follow-up email, and gives you a clean, readable transcript alongside the raw version.

5

It prepares you and remembers everything.

Before your next call it builds a prep brief from past meetings and your own documents, and at any time you can search or ask a question across everything you have recorded.

What it does

Local Meeting Manager does far more than record and transcribe your calls.

Each capability is grounded in the meetings you have actually recorded, and every one of them runs from your own Mac.

🎙️

It captures the call without a bot joining.

Recording starts automatically when a Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or FaceTime window opens, from a calendar event, or on demand, capturing both your mic and the system audio.

🗣️

Speaker identification learns each voice once.

The app maps voices to real names and recognizes the same person in every later meeting, with a quiet amber dot flagging any label worth a second glance.

📋

Your prep brief is ready before the call starts.

A pre-meeting brief gathers the attendees, the most relevant past meetings, open action items, and agenda hints, and a Daily Brief puts your whole day on one page.

🔎

You can ask one question across every meeting.

Ask Anything answers questions like what you decided about pricing last quarter, and ⌘K search spans transcripts, summaries, people, action items, and captured slides.

👥

Everyone you meet becomes a lasting profile.

A people directory keeps each person's aliases, voice, organization, and full meeting history, and a companies view rolls up open items by email domain.

📊

You can see how your meetings actually go.

Talk-time, meeting load, topic trackers, and neutral relationship signals are computed on your Mac from transcripts you already have, with no AI model required.

Your choice of AI

You choose whether the AI runs in the cloud with your key or entirely on your Mac.

There is no Local Meeting Manager cloud in the middle, and you can switch between the two options or mix them at any time.

Cloud, with your own key

You add a Claude API key when you want the highest-quality summaries.

What leavesThe relevant transcript slice and your prompt go to Anthropic over HTTPS; raw audio and voice fingerprints never do.
What it costsAbout five to eight cents for a 30-minute meeting, billed to your own key.
How fastSummaries come back in seconds, even on long meetings.
Local, on your Mac

You run a local model so that no meeting content ever leaves your machine.

What leavesNothing, because the model runs over localhost on your own Mac.
What it costsNothing beyond the app itself; the local model is free to run.
How fastSummaries take roughly thirty seconds to a few minutes, depending on your hardware.
