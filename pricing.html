Your choice of AI

You choose whether the AI runs in the cloud with your key or entirely on your Mac.

The app is the same either way; only the model that writes your summaries changes. You can switch between the two at any time, and even mix them.

On the cloud side, the app defaults to Claude Sonnet for a strong balance of speed and quality, with a faster Haiku option for routine calls and a higher-quality Opus option for long or important meetings. On the local side, the app can install a fast model and a larger one for longer meetings; the local runtime lives privately inside the app's own support folder and runs headless, so there is no separate app or menu-bar item for you to manage. You can run cheap tasks locally and send only the final summary to Claude, so the choice is never all-or-nothing.

A typical 30-minute meeting costs about five to eight cents when you use Claude, and nothing at all when you run the local model.

You can decide between local and cloud after you have seen both on your own meetings.
